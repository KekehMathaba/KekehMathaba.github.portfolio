About me.

Why I chose Information Technology

I chose Information Technology because I’m interested in how technology can turn ideas and everyday needs into useful digital solutions. My studies let me explore how software is designed and how people use it.

Career goals

My goal is to keep building a strong foundation for a career in software development. I want to become more confident solving practical problems, creating dependable solutions and working as part of an IT team.

Software development is my main career interest. I’m also exploring cloud computing and AWS, automation, DevOps and artificial intelligence. My current development areas include:
